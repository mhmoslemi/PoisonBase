\subsection{Ablation on Robustness to Data Augmentation}
\label{sec:augmentation-robustness}

Table~\ref{tab:augmentation-robustness} tests whether the base-selection
advantage persists when the victim uses data augmentation.  Poisons are
created once for each selector and then held fixed while only victim training
changes, isolating the effect of augmentation.  The expanded sweep uses the
same dog--bird configurations as Table~\ref{tab:defense-extra}, adds Greedy and
the lower-temperature DPP variants ($\alpha\in\{0.25,0.1\}$), and evaluates
each completed cell on five paired targets and four victims per target.

Among the currently completed Random versus DPP ($\alpha=2$) comparisons, DPP
achieves higher ASR in 15 of 16 cells and ties in one, with a mean paired
improvement of $24.4\%$.  The advantage persists under Crop+Flip,
RandAugment, and Cutout.  Interestingly, stronger poisoning objectives such as
GM can benefit from victim-side augmentation when combined with DPP-selected
bases.  For example, Crop+Flip raises DPP ASR from $55.0\%$ to $70.0\%$ at
$\epsilon=2{\times}10^{-3}$ while also improving clean performance.  The new
cells test whether this behavior generalizes across architectures, attacks,
budgets, and more concentrated DPP selections.
